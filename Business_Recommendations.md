## Business Recommendations

### 1. Increase Investment in Organic Search

Organic Search generates the highest volume of leads and revenue, making it the most effective acquisition channel. Expanding SEO efforts and improving organic visibility may further increase high-quality lead acquisition.

### 2. Optimize Paid Search Campaigns

Paid Search is a major contributor to both traffic and revenue. Regular campaign optimization, keyword analysis, and budget reallocation toward high-performing campaigns may improve return on marketing investment.

### 3. Reduce Sales Cycle Duration

The average time between lead acquisition and deal closure remains relatively high. Identifying bottlenecks in the sales process and improving lead follow-up strategies may help accelerate conversions and improve operational efficiency.

### 4. Focus on High-Value Customer Segments

Manufacturers generate significantly higher revenue per closed deal compared to resellers. Increasing efforts to attract and retain manufacturers could improve overall revenue performance.

### 5. Prioritize Top-Performing Business Segments

Business segments such as construction_tools_house_garden, phone_mobile, home_decor, pet, and health_beauty consistently generate the highest revenue. These segments should be prioritized when allocating marketing and sales resources.

### 6. Review Underperforming Segments

Segments including watches, food_drink, perfume, party, and bed_bath_table contribute relatively low revenue. Further investigation is needed to determine whether these segments require additional support, repositioning, or reduced investment.
